PixelBlitz

A free pixel-art workbench from Ghost Meridian. Turn any image into pixel art, then draw, rig, animate and build whole casts of characters for your game, in one browser page.

See what it does

No install and no account: it runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and works offline too. Follow Ghost Meridian to hear the moment it’s ready.

Convert

Any image in, pixel art out

Drop, paste or open a picture, a photo, a sketch or a screenshot, and pick a grid. Pixel Blitz samples it into clean cells and a tidy palette you can keep editing.

  • Grids from 1 to 256 cells, with presets from 16² to 128²
  • Detect grid finds the real pixel size of upscaled or resized pixel art, even odd sizes like 7.3 px, and gives you back one cell per pixel
  • Automatic palettes, Lospec presets, PICO-8, or your own .hex or .gpl file
  • Ordered or Floyd–Steinberg dither, clean-up, outlines and background removal
Draw · Rig · Animate

Paint it, pose it, play it

Layers, a pixel-perfect pen and a palette that offers each colour's natural highlight and shadow. Give a sprite bones and pose it instead of redrawing every frame: parts turn cleanly and keep their outlines. Then lay out frames and tags with onion skin, and export straight into your game.

  • Alt + scroll resizes the brush; hold Alt to pick a colour and go straight back to painting
  • Bones, inverse kinematics, mirrored poses and in-betweens, with hand fixes that stay on the part
  • GIF, PNG sheets and Godot SpriteFrames, one animation per tag
  • Versions (V1, V2…) side by side on a board, so you can try ideas without losing the original
Portrait

Faces from parts, lit from any side

Build characters from Library parts: heads, hair, eyes, mouths and accessories. Every part can have mood drawings, every colour role has variants, and Shuffle rolls a whole squad with no two alike.

Turn on lighting and the portrait is shaded from the direction you choose, with cast shadows, light along the shoulders and an optional dither. The parts themselves never change, so you draw them flat and let the light do the shading.

Library

One kit for every project

Every part you save is shared across your projects. Edit one and every portrait using it updates. Group colours into roles, recolour the whole Library at once, or see it through any palette as a look, without touching a pixel.

  • Moods with fallbacks: a part with no angry drawing borrows its neutral one
  • Match moments map game events like shooting to the right mood
  • Export the kit for Godot and generate characters while the game runs
In your game

The same pixels in Godot

The Godot character kit builds faces from the same parts, moods, colours and lighting, so what you see in Pixel Blitz is what your players see.

With Claude

Hand Claude the busywork

Press Connect Claude and Claude can read and edit your sprites, import frames, build portraits and export into your project. It runs on your own Claude account, and Pixel Blitz never asks for an API key.
